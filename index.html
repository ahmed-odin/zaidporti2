I’m Elias.
I collect quiet.

Photographer, occasional wanderer, and chronic taker of the long way home. Based in Copenhagen, usually somewhere else.

I’m drawn to the in-between: the hour before sunrise, the last lit window on a dark street, the strange softness that arrives when a place is almost empty. Shooting mostly on film, always by feeling.
